I'm Sorry 🤍

I'm sorry because you matter to me, not because I expect anything from you.

I want to be completely open and accountable. If my words, my silence, or my actions have ever caused you pain, disappointment, or feeling unheard, I am truly sorry from the bottom of my heart. There are no excuses, no deflections, and no attempts to justify what hurt you.

Your peace of mind and your emotional safety mean too much to me for me to ever treat our bond lightly. I recognize that apologies without growth are hollow, so my commitment is to listen better, speak with more gentleness, and understand your perspective before defending my own.

You do not owe me forgiveness, nor do I ask for it to ease my conscience. My only goal is to acknowledge what you felt, validate it with total respect, and let you know that our connection will always be something I protect with sincerity and care.

✓ Unconditional respect for your boundaries
